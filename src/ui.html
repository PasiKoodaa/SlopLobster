Experimental thinking mode
Bundles extra reasoning strategies on top of update_beliefs: invariant tracking (declare constraints that must keep holding across a multi-step task, kept visible in the system prompt the whole time), premortems (optional "if this breaks, most likely reason" note attached to commits/risky commands, shown in the confirmation), and reflexion (a forced one-line self-critique before retrying a failed test, instead of silently retrying). Adds real per-turn overhead — worth it for gnarly/high-stakes work, not routine tasks.
